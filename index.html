Kyulee Jeon

Ph.D. Candidate in Biomedical Systems Informatics, Yonsei University College of Medicine

I make multimodal health data interoperable so that clinical AI can be validated and adopted in routine care, advised by Prof. Seng Chan You. I link multimodal medical images with electronic health records through a standardized common data model, and I generate evidence for clinical AI through multi-institutional, distributed validation.

I am a co-author of the Medical Imaging CDM, the imaging extension for the OMOP Common Data Model, and an active member of the OHDSI community and its Medical Imaging Working Group. I expect to graduate in August 2027.

J Biomed Inform 2026 · co-first author

ARKE: An Ontology-Driven Framework for Automated Mapping of Local Radiology Procedure Terms to the LOINC-RadLex Playbook Using Large Language Model

Sumin Lee*, Kyulee Jeon*, Joon Seok Lim, Yiju Park, Min Seong Kim, Ju Hyun Jin, Chang Hoon Han, Soon Ho Yoon, Seng Chan You

  • Why it matters: multi-institutional validation of imaging AI needs the exam name that links imaging exams to patient data, but each hospital writes it differently.
  • Mapping as knowledge encoding: ARKE decomposes each local name into Playbook attributes, retrieves the top 10 candidates from the ontology, and confines the LLM to attribute parsing and final selection. No codes are invented.
  • Result: across two tertiary hospitals in Korea (3,326 procedure names), recall@1 of 0.70 (internal) and 0.81 (external), more than 20 points above direct code-name mapping.
